# Glass visual reference Bible

Glass is a material response, not a body colour. The M6 study should use
lighting, wall thickness, front/rear separation, rim, and base cues to make the
apparatus readable.

## GOOD

- Neutral or very low-saturation body response with stronger contrast at
  structural edges.
- A rim with several readable surfaces: rear edge, opening, front lip, and a
  small thickness cue.
- A bottom that catches a restrained broad highlight because it is physically
  thicker than the wall.
- Asymmetric highlights that describe a studio environment rather than a
  repeated full-height stripe.
- Light and dark background variants that remain legible without switching to
  an opaque cartoon outline.
- Subtle front/rear wall separation that makes the object feel hollow.

## BAD

- Entire body filled with saturated cyan or blue-green. This encodes “glass” as
  a flat icon colour and hides the optical structure.
- A thick white contour around every edge. It works on one background but fails
  as a physical cue and becomes an icon border.
- A continuous white highlight from rim to base. It makes a cylinder look like
  plastic and removes local structure.
- Strong HDRI sparkle or dramatic caustics that compete with graduations and
  later dynamic liquid layers.
- High transmission with no edge or background control. The object disappears
  on the light review background.

## Observe

The agent should inspect the relationship between:

- wall thickness and edge contrast;
- rim thickness and opening readability;
- base thickness and visual grounding;
- background contrast and transparency;
- reflection-card width and highlight width;
- roughness/transmission and the ability to read a small silhouette.

## Do not copy

Do not copy manufacturer logos, exact product photography reflections, labels,
or another virtual laboratory's glass shader. Use the references to understand
what surfaces do, then author ChemRealm's own material treatment.

## Review views

Every glass candidate is reviewed in full-size light, full-size dark,
thumbnail light, thumbnail dark, close-up rim, close-up spout, and alpha
preview. A candidate that only works under one background is not selected.

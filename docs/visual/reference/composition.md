# Composition and review-view reference Bible

The M6 review composition is orthographic and deliberately diagnostic. It must
show the apparatus clearly before any later scene composition adds liquid,
hands, clamps, labels, or interaction overlays.

## Review views

- `front-light`: primary shape and marking read on a light-neutral background;
- `front-dark`: edge and wall separation on a dark-neutral background;
- `thumbnail-light` and `thumbnail-dark`: silhouette and visual mass only;
- `closeup-rim`: rim thickness, opening, and rear/front wall separation;
- `closeup-spout`: continuity from rim/body into the pour lip;
- `alpha-check`: transparent export behavior without a baked background.

## GOOD

- The object has enough negative space to read its full silhouette.
- The camera and framing remain fixed across candidates in a matrix.
- Close-ups answer a specific structural question rather than serving as
  decorative beauty shots.
- The thumbnail still identifies the apparatus without relying on text.

## BAD

- Perspective convergence that makes candidates incomparable.
- Cropping the base or rim to make a weak silhouette appear larger.
- Changing camera, background, material, and shape simultaneously.
- A contact sheet with no candidate IDs, variable labels, or backend metadata.

## Benchmark boundary

Public NOBOOK material is used to understand the importance of discoverable,
readable, manipulable apparatus in a coherent virtual laboratory. It is not a
pixel or layout specification. ChemRealm must preserve its own Observable,
World, privacy, and scientific contracts.
